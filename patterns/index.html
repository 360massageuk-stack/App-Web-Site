What Are Taekwon-Do Patterns?

Patterns, also known as tul, are a fundamental part of ITF Taekwon-Do. They combine attacking and defensive techniques into structured sequences designed to develop balance, coordination, power, technique and understanding.

Students progress through different patterns as their grade and experience increase. Each pattern has its own name, sequence and significance within the Taekwon-Do syllabus.

The TKD APP brings pattern training together with theory, terminology, quizzes, grading preparation and technical resources, helping students continue learning between classes.
