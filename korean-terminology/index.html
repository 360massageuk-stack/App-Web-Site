Learn the Korean words and commands used in ITF Taekwon-Do. Understanding terminology helps students follow instruction, prepare for gradings and develop a deeper understanding of the art.

Why Learn Korean Terminology?

Korean terminology is an important part of Taekwon-Do training. Students hear Korean words throughout classes, patterns, gradings and competitions.

Learning the language used for commands, techniques and training helps students understand instruction more quickly and builds confidence as they progress through the grades.

This guide covers useful terminology students are likely to hear regularly. The TKD APP contains further theory, pattern and grading resources for continued study between classes.
