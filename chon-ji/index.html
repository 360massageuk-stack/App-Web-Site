ITF Taekwon-Do Pattern Guide

Chon-Ji

Learn the meaning and structure of Chon-Ji, the first pattern in the ITF Taekwon-Do pattern system and an important foundation for students beginning their pattern training.

01 First Pattern
19 Movements
Chon-Ji Heaven & Earth
Pattern One

What Is Chon-Ji?

Chon-Ji is the first pattern learned in the ITF Taekwon-Do pattern system. It introduces students to the discipline of performing a structured sequence of fundamental movements against imaginary opponents.

The pattern contains 19 movements and gives students an early opportunity to develop direction, balance, stance control, coordination and accurate basic technique.

Learning the sequence is only the beginning. As students improve, attention should also be given to posture, correct stance, breathing, timing, power and the quality of every technique.

Meaning Of Chon-Ji
“The Heaven and the Earth”

Chon-Ji literally means the Heaven and the Earth. In the traditional interpretation of the pattern, this represents the creation of the world or the beginning of human history.

This makes Chon-Ji especially appropriate as the first pattern studied by a beginner — the starting point of their journey through the ITF pattern system.

Training Chon-Ji

What Should Beginners Focus On?

Chon-Ji may be the first pattern, but developing good habits here creates a strong foundation for every pattern that follows.

01

Learn The Sequence

Know where each movement goes before trying to add speed. Accuracy and confidence should come first.

02

Watch Your Stances

Correct stance length, width and weight distribution help create balance and strong technique.

03

Finish Each Technique

Avoid rushing from one movement into the next. Each technique should reach a clear and controlled finishing position.

04

Use Both Sides

Patterns help students develop coordination and technique on both sides of the body rather than relying on a favourite side.

05

Control Your Breathing

Good breathing supports timing, focus and the application of power throughout the pattern.

06

Return With Control

Finishing a pattern properly matters. Maintain concentration until the performance is fully complete.

Grading Preparation

Preparing Chon-Ji For Grading

Know The Pattern Name

Be able to identify Chon-Ji confidently and understand the meaning associated with the pattern.

Know The Movement Count

Chon-Ji consists of 19 movements. Students should be able to perform the complete sequence without relying on someone else.

Practise Direction

Pattern practice should develop awareness of direction and help the student move confidently through the prescribed sequence.

Improve The Basics

Examiners are not simply looking for somebody who remembers the order. Stances, technique, control and understanding matter too.

Practise Without Following

Once the sequence is familiar, practise independently so you know the pattern rather than copying another student.

Exact grading requirements can vary between Taekwon-Do schools and organisations. Always follow the technical instruction and syllabus provided by your own instructor.
