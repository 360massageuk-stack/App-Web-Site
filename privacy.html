ELITE TKD Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

ELITE TKD is a Taekwon-Do training application intended for adults aged 18 and over.

Information and Access

The current version uses a shared access password. Users do not create individual accounts or enter their name or email address into the app.

Information Stored on Your Device

The app stores certain settings locally, including access status and training preferences, to remember your choices between sessions.

External Services

The app may display training videos provided by YouTube. Viewing these videos may involve data processing by Google under its own privacy policies.

Subscriptions

We intend to offer subscriptions through Google Play. Before subscriptions are launched, this policy will be updated to explain the final payment and access arrangements.

Data Retention

Locally stored app settings may be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app, depending on device backup settings.

Your Privacy Rights

You may contact us to ask questions about your personal information or exercise applicable privacy rights.
