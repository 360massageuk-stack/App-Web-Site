Do-San

Learn the meaning and key training points of Do-San, the third pattern in the ITF Taekwon-Do pattern system.

Pattern Three

What Is Do-San?

Do-San is the third pattern in the ITF Taekwon-Do pattern system, following Chon-Ji and Dan-Gun in a student's progression.

The pattern contains 24 movements and continues the development of fundamental technique, balance, direction, stance control and coordination.

As students progress into Do-San, the aim is not simply to remember the order of the movements. Technique should become more controlled, confident and purposeful with every repetition.

Meaning Of Do-San
The Patriot Ahn Chang-Ho

Do-San is the pseudonym of the patriot Ahn Chang-Ho (1876–1938).

The 24 movements represent his entire life, which he devoted to furthering the education of Korea and its independence movement.

Learning the meaning behind a pattern helps connect technical Taekwon-Do training with the history represented within the ITF pattern system.

Training Do-San

Developing Your Technique

Do-San builds on the foundations developed in Chon-Ji and Dan-Gun while asking the student to perform a longer sequence with confidence and control.

01

Know All 24 Movements

Learn the full sequence until you can perform it without depending on another student for direction.

02

Keep Strong Stances

Check your stance length, width, weight distribution and posture throughout the pattern.

03

Control Your Direction

Stay aware of where you are moving and avoid rushing changes of direction or turns.

04

Finish Each Technique

Give each block, punch, strike and thrust a clear finishing position before moving on.

05

Use Breathing

Coordinate breathing with your movements to support timing, concentration and power.

06

Train With Purpose

Slow the pattern down when necessary. Good practice is about improving technique, not simply reaching the end.

Grading Preparation

Preparing Do-San For Grading

Know The Meaning

Be able to identify Do-San as the pseudonym of Ahn Chang-Ho and understand what the 24 movements represent.

Remember 24 Movements

Do-San contains 24 movements. Practise until you can perform the sequence confidently and independently.

Maintain Balance

Keep control during turns, transitions and changes of stance. Avoid allowing speed to affect your balance.

Improve Technical Quality

Concentrate on stance, posture, correct finishing positions and the quality of each individual technique.

Keep Earlier Patterns Sharp

Continue practising Chon-Ji and Dan-Gun alongside Do-San. Earlier patterns remain part of your training as you progress.

Exact grading requirements can vary between Taekwon-Do schools and organisations. Always follow the technical instruction and syllabus provided by your own instructor.
